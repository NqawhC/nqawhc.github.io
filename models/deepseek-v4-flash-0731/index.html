DeepSeek V4 Flash (0731)

The workhorse that held the daily-driver slot until GLM narrowly took it: a sparse-attention MoE that holds its decode speed flat across the whole context window, and, after a July re-post-train, one of the most reliable local models on the benchmark.

What to run

The familiar fallback now that GLM leads on score, and still the easier one to stand up: it serves on a stock vLLM build with no custom image, holds a flat decode, and sits a notch behind GLM rather than a tier. Serve it with DSpark speculative decode configured correctly, and A/B spec-on against spec-off on real tasks before you trust it, because a subtly wrong build costs quality at the same speed.

The re-post-train moved capability around

The July re-post-train lifts the hard-task average from about 2.0 to about 2.5, but not as a flat bonus. One task jumped from not trying at all to a solid fix, another stepped up a clear notch, a third genuinely regressed right at the edge of the noise, and the fourth was already at the ceiling. A re-post-train moves the model around in capability space rather than adding a constant; here the gains outweigh the one real loss.

Flat decode, and what the speed actually buys

Sparse MLA means most layers do not care how long the context is, so decode stays roughly flat - about 180 to 255 tok/s - the whole way out, where dense models collapse. DSpark speculative decode is what you serve with, but its rate is content-dependent, so the honest speedup over no-spec is roughly 1.4x to 2.2x, not the ~2.6x a repetitive-filler taper flatters it into. And decode is not the clock you wait on: an agentic run is mostly prefill and tool round-trips, so the end-to-end win lands around 1.3x to 1.9x.

Serving gotchas

  • The speculative-decode trap
    DSpark is lossless only if the build verifies drafts by exact rejection sampling. A build that verified approximately, and a block size of 7 where the checkpoint wants 5, each quietly cost quality at the same ~290 tok/s. A/B spec-on vs spec-off on real tasks - perplexity will not catch a gap this small; eight graded runs did.
  • Its taper wobble is spec variance, not a curve
    DSpark acceptance depends on how predictable the next tokens are, so the decode line wiggles. Measured on a diverse corpus it is flat ~180-255; a filler taper reads a rising, impossible 183 to 347.
